About Pet Nova

PET

What it means

Represents our greatest source of inspiration: those special beings who, with their companionship, affection and loyalty, become a fundamental part of our lives. They are the ones who motivate us to create a space designed for their well-being and happiness.

🐱

NOVA

What it means

Refers to a nova, an astronomical phenomenon in which a star dramatically increases its brightness. It symbolizes the special light our pets bring to our lives, brightening our days with their presence, joy and unconditional love.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make living with your pet easier. At Pet Nova we strive to improve your pet’s quality of life.

We know it’s often not easy to stay on top of everything, but we are the solution!

More than just an app

We are guardians of your pet and a family united by our love. Every feature in Pet Nova, from the medical history to Paw Alert, was created to give you peace of mind and more time for what matters: enjoying life with your best friend.
