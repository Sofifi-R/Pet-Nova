Pet Nova

The app that helps you care for your pet's health and safety. Keep their medical history, receive vaccine reminders, activate Paw Alert in emergencies, and find them on the map, all from your mobile phone.

We believe every pet deserves the best start. At Pet Nova, we develop tools to help you care for their health, well-being, and happiness, accompanying you during every stage of their lives.

Everything for your pet's well-being

Everything your favorite pet needs, on a single platform.

Paw Alert

If your pet gets lost, activate the alert and notify the community and nearby vets in seconds, with their photo and last known location.

Pet Map

Find vets, services and pet-friendly places near you, and check your pet’s last recorded location.

Medical History

Keep vaccines, appointments, treatments and allergies in one place, ready to show your vet whenever you need them.

Smart Calendar

Get reminders for vaccines, deworming, baths and walks so you never miss an important date.

Community

Connect with other pet owners, share tips and help find lost pets in your area.
